I

Every credible scholarly discipline eventually builds structure around its inquiry. For UAP research, that time is now: AURA/UPWARD gives SUAPS a public, legible, and accountable system for turning isolated research ideas into reviewed projects, stewarded outputs, and integrated knowledge.

The initiative

AURA/UPWARD is not an acronym wall. It is the operating logic for research.

AURA/UPWARD was created to address a persistent problem in UAP research: promising work can remain isolated, unevenly documented, difficult to fund, or disconnected from later learning. AURA supplies the intake and governance structure. UPWARD supplies the integration layer that helps findings remain visible, comparable, and useful over time.

AURA is the operational pathway. It helps SUAPS determine where a proposal belongs, what documentation is needed, whether the work is feasible and rigorous, and how a project should be tracked if it advances. It is selective by design: a submission is an invitation to review, not an automatic endorsement or funding commitment.

UPWARD keeps the larger intellectual horizon in view. Research should not disappear into scattered reports or isolated project files. When AURA-governed work produces outputs, UPWARD supports synthesis, institutional memory, and the longer effort to build a credible field of UAP Studies.

Two functions, one system

AURA vets and governs. UPWARD connects and integrates.

The public page should keep this distinction crisp. AURA is the proposal and project pathway. UPWARD is the synthesis and institutional-learning layer.

AURA

The operational engine

AURA manages proposal intake, phase assignment, review, decision records, documentation expectations, reporting, and research-project governance.

  • Receives structured proposals.
  • Clarifies required documents by phase.
  • Assesses merit, method, feasibility, alignment, impact, and team readiness.
  • Tracks projects through execution and closeout.
UPWARD

The synthesis layer

UPWARD connects what SUAPS learns across projects, disciplines, methods, and outputs. Its purpose is integration rather than intake.

  • Supports cross-project synthesis.
  • Preserves institutional memory.
  • Builds toward an Institutional Knowledge Map.
  • Helps translate findings for scholarly and public use.
Lifecycle discipline

The public AURA pathway uses five phases.

The lifecycle is adapted for research projects of different sizes, disciplines, and PI profiles. Not every proposal begins at the same point. Smaller or more mature projects may enter later; larger or less-developed projects may begin earlier.

Phase 0

Conceptual Design

Early concept development for projects needing a clearer research question, team, budget, or feasibility picture.

Typical document: concept outline
Phase 1

Preliminary Design

Preliminary proposal development, including feasibility, methodology, budget, timeline, risks, and partners.

Typical document: white paper
Phase 2

Final Design

A full proposal dossier ready for substantive review, donor-readiness consideration, and possible project formation.

Typical document: full proposal
Phase 3

Research Execution

The research is conducted according to the approved plan, with milestones, reporting, outputs, and appropriate oversight.

Typical output: progress reporting
Phase 4

Final Synthesis & Integration

Findings, publications, public summaries, reporting, and closeout materials are integrated into SUAPS institutional learning.

Typical output: final report
For principal investigators

Choose the right entry point before you submit.

AURA scales its expectations to project maturity, budget, and PI experience. When in doubt, researchers may submit conservatively and allow SUAPS reviewers to advise on the correct phase.

Project profile Likely entry point Typical submission Primary question
Early concept, large budget, non-academic PI, or first-time grant lead Phase 0 - Conceptual Design Concept Outline / Letter of Intent Is the idea coherent, relevant, and promising enough to develop further?
Developing project with substantial budget or unresolved technical/design questions Phase 1 - Preliminary Design White Paper / Preliminary Proposal Is the plan feasible, methodologically credible, and ready for final proposal work?
Mature project with experienced PI and a defined team, budget, method, and outputs Phase 2 - Final Design Full Proposal Is the project ready for formal review, portfolio consideration, and possible support?

UPWARD kept intact

The point is not only to approve projects. It is to learn from them.

AURA governs research as it enters and moves through SUAPS. UPWARD explains why that structure matters: each project should contribute to a larger body of knowledge rather than disappear into a one-off report.

As AURA-funded or SUAPS-affiliated projects produce outputs, UPWARD supports cross-project synthesis, thematic analysis, institutional memory, and future public-facing knowledge tools such as an Institutional Knowledge Map.

This page keeps UPWARD present but not overburdened. The immediate public need is a clear AURA submission and review pathway; the companion promise is that SUAPS will integrate what it learns.
