Architecture of Discovery

A real page for the core explanation.

AURA structures the work. UPWARD integrates what the work reveals.

This page replaces the old modal-style explainer with a dedicated, readable, indexable account of how the AURA/UPWARD system is meant to function for researchers, partners, and institutional stakeholders.

Why structure matters

AURA/UPWARD is SUAPS's answer to fragmentation.

UAP research has often lacked a shared public pathway for scoping, evaluating, funding, executing, and integrating serious work. AURA/UPWARD is designed to make that process more disciplined without flattening the diversity of questions, methods, and disciplines that UAP Studies requires.

AURA gives proposals a route: concept, preliminary design, final proposal, execution, and closeout. UPWARD gives results a destination: synthesis, institutional memory, and eventual knowledge mapping.

The architecture is intentionally practical. It helps researchers understand what SUAPS needs to review; it helps SUAPS steward projects with accountability; and it helps partners see how isolated proposals can become part of a cumulative research portfolio.

Five-phase lifecycle

From concept to public synthesis.

AURA uses a five-phase lifecycle adapted from leading aerospace and science-research funding practices. It is scaled to project maturity, budget, and investigator experience.

Phase 0

Conceptual Design

Curiosity-driven and exploratory ideas take root here. A concept outline clarifies objectives, collaborators, rough budget, timeline, relevance, and feasibility. This phase is generative and is especially useful for large, early, non-academic, or first-time PI projects.

Phase 1

Preliminary Design

Projects with substantial budget or unresolved design questions submit a white paper or preliminary proposal. Review focuses on novelty, methodology, feasibility, risks, budget, timeline, and team competence.

Phase 2

Final Design

All projects ultimately require a full proposal. SUAPS assesses rigor, team capacity, strategic fit, data management, budget, deliverables, and public or scholarly impact before a project is considered ready for support or portfolio presentation.

Phase 3

Research Execution

The core research is conducted according to the approved plan. The project produces progress reports, publications, datasets, public outputs, or other agreed deliverables while remaining accountable to the approved scope.

Phase 4

Final Synthesis and Integration

Research findings are synthesized, made public where appropriate, and integrated into SUAPS institutional learning. UPWARD helps connect project outputs to broader patterns, questions, and future research pathways.

Key distinction

AURA is selective and operational. UPWARD is integrative and institutional.

This distinction should appear consistently across the site.

AURA

Selective and operational

AURA decides whether a proposal is sufficiently aligned, feasible, rigorous, and accountable to advance within SUAPS's research pathway.

UPWARD

Integrative and institutional

UPWARD ensures that findings do not remain isolated. It supports synthesis, field learning, and the long-term intellectual memory of SUAPS.
